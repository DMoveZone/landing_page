ZONE 1 (8-10): PEAK PERFORMANCE

What DMove does for you?

  • APP: Track your performance.
  • DMove Manager: Do the testing day with him.
  • DMove HUB: Connect with our specialist if you and Dmove manager think is the good choice for you and learn with the academy as much as you can.
ZONE 2 (6-8): FUNCTIONAL BUFFER

What DMove does for you?

  • APP: Monitor your lifestyle (trainings, recovery and mind)
  • DMove Manager: guide you to avoid going to injury risk or directly to the injury.
  • DMove HUB: Connect with our specialist if you and Dmove manager think is the good choice for you and learn with the academy as much as you can.
ZONE 3 (3-6): EARLY WARNING

What DMove does for you?

  • APP: 3 mins triage that monitor and interpret your symptoms and capacities.
  • DMove manager: Guide you with some tips to improve and avoid going to the injury phase.
  • DMove HUB: Connect with our specialist if you and Dmove manager think is the good choice for you and learn with the academy as much as you can.
ZONE 4 (0-3): MEDICAL

What DMove does for you?

  • DMove HUB Network: Direct, friction-free access to top paramedical experts.
  • Personalized Recovery: Tailored rehabilitation programs driven by your profile.
  • Clinical Continuity: Your physiological history is delivered directly to your doctor.

DMoveZone es el único ecosistema tecnológico y humano diseñado para protegerte y mejorarte en las fases Peak y Early Warnings. Actuamos en la sombra para que nunca tengas que visitar la fase Injured.
